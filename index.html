LinkScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

LinkScope is a Chrome extension that crawls a website you choose and visualizes its internal linking structure as an interactive graph, along with SEO diagnostics such as click depth, orphan pages, broken links, and anchor-text analysis. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

The short version: LinkScope runs locally in your browser. It does not track your browsing, show ads, or sell your data. The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to use the optional AI analysis feature, which sends a summary of the crawled site to an AI provider to generate link suggestions.

What the extension does with data

When you start a crawl, LinkScope fetches the pages of the website you point it at and reads their content locally to build the link graph. This includes page URLs, titles, meta descriptions, headings, word counts, and the internal links (and their anchor text) between pages. This processing happens entirely on your device.

Local storage

Your most recent crawl results and your settings are saved locally on your device using Chrome's storage API so the extension can restore them without re-crawling. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time by discarding the saved crawl or removing the extension.

Optional AI analysis

LinkScope includes an optional feature that can generate internal-linking suggestions using an AI model. If — and only if — you run this feature, the extension sends a compact summary of the crawled site to the configured AI provider (by default, Anthropic's API). That summary may include page URLs, titles, meta descriptions, top keywords, and internal link counts for the site being analyzed. It does not include your personal information or your browsing history.

The data sent is processed by the AI provider under their own privacy terms solely to return suggestions to you. If you never use the AI feature, no website data is ever transmitted off your device. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

What we do NOT collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, age, IDs).
  • No health, financial, or authentication information.
  • No location data.
  • No tracking of your browsing history or activity across sites.
  • No analytics, advertising, or behavioral profiling.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. We do not transfer your data to third parties except as strictly necessary to provide the optional AI feature you explicitly invoke, as described above. We do not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Data retention

Crawl results and settings remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the extension. We do not operate any server that stores your data.
